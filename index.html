Our work
01 / Who we are

A good education should help you live a better life.

Hilth Foundation is a nonprofit organisation helping young people understand what education really is. We connect learning with real life, purpose and sustainable growth, so that education becomes useful to the person and to the community. Our headquarters is in Ghana, with a vision to support young people across Africa.
