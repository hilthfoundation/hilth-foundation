01

HILTH School Impact Program

A school-outreach initiative engaging students through interactive and age-appropriate sessions designed to go beyond lectures.

Structured around four core modules:

i. Education Beyond Exams

Helping students understand the difference between schooling and true education, and why thinking matters more than memorization.

ii. Self-Discovery

Guiding students to identify their interests, strengths, and natural inclinations.

iii. Skills Exposure

Introducing modern skills, STEAM concepts, and real-world problem-solving.

iv. Direction Setting

Supporting students to connect self-awareness and skills to clear next steps and achievable goals.

All sessions are interactive, reflective, and designed to actively engage students.

02

HILTH Mentorship Program

A structured mentorship system supporting students and young people in their purpose, career, self-development, skills development, and academic pursuits.

Through trained mentors, participants receive practical guidance, accountability, and exposure to make informed decisions about school, work, and life beyond exams and certificates.

  • Free for Senior High School students
  • Paid for tertiary students & young professionals
  • Enrollment requires a short application form
