HILTH School Impact Programme

We visit schools for interactive, age-appropriate sessions where students explore ideas, practise problem-solving and consider their next steps.

Structured around four core modules:

i. Education Beyond Exams

Helping students understand the difference between schooling and true education, and why thinking matters more than memorisation.

ii. Self-Discovery

Guiding students to identify their interests, strengths, and natural inclinations.

iii. Skills Exposure

Introducing modern skills, STEAM concepts, and real-world problem-solving.

iv. Direction Setting

Supporting students to connect self-awareness and skills to clear next steps and achievable goals.

All sessions are interactive, reflective, and designed to actively engage students.
