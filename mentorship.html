Guidance for a whole life.

A proposed 12-month, whole-life mentoring programme for Senior High School and university students, designed to help each mentee build a practical Life Plan for their next step.

Planned pilot in Kumasi · Enrolment has not begun

The format below describes the proposed programme. Launch depends on programme approval and safeguarding arrangements being in place. Interest registration does not confirm a place.

Who it is for

Senior High School students, and university and other tertiary students.

The proposed mentoring year

Mentoring circles

SHS students would meet in circles with one lead mentor, two near-peer mentors and eight mentees.

One-to-one guidance

Tertiary students would be matched with a professional mentor. Monthly specialist masterclasses are planned for the whole group.

Online and in Kumasi

Most mentoring is planned online, with a meet-up each term at partner venues in Kumasi, so travel need not be a barrier.

A practical next step

Each mentee would finish with a practical Life Plan. A Certificate of Completion is planned after the mentoring year.

Cost and access

The proposed programme is free for SHS students. Tertiary students may pay an optional token fee; waivers are planned with no questions asked.

Safeguarding before launch

Safe by design

The proposed safeguards require every mentor to be vetted and trained before meeting any student. For students under 18, contact would happen in monitored group channels with guardian and school permission. Assessment of concerns within 24 hours is part of the proposed policy.

These commitments depend on approval of the plan, appointment of a Safeguarding Lead and adoption of the safeguarding policy.

Want to mentor?

Mentors would be people aged 18 or over, above SHS level, who can give 3 to 5 hours a month for a year. Vetting and training would be required before meeting students.
