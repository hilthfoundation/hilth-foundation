Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Hilth Foundation ("we," "our," or "us") is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting education as a foundation for development across Africa. We are committed to protecting your privacy when you visit our website, participate in our programs, or support our mission.

2. Information We Collect

Mentorship Program Applications

When you apply for our Mentorship Program, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Age range and educational level
  • School or institution name
  • Areas of interest (purpose, career, self-development, skills, academics)
  • WhatsApp number and email address

Donations

When you make a donation, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Donation amount

Important: We do NOT collect, process, or store your payment card information. All payment processing is handled securely by Paystack, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor.

Newsletter Subscriptions

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address to send updates about our programs and impact.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match mentees with appropriate mentors and facilitate program participation
  • Process donations and send receipts
  • Send program updates, newsletters, and impact reports
  • Communicate about HSIP school outreach sessions
  • Distribute the 90 Days of Transformation notebook orders
  • Improve our programs and website experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Data Sharing

With Mentors

If you apply for our Mentorship Program, your application information may be shared with assigned mentors to facilitate the mentoring relationship.

Payment Processing

Donation payments are processed through Paystack. Their privacy policy is available at: paystack.com/privacy

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your information for commercial marketing without consent

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your personal information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure payment processing through PCI-DSS certified providers
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security reviews of our systems

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdrawal: Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies for essential functionality only. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Your browsing behavior is not monitored or shared with external parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Mentorship records: Duration of the program plus 2 years
  • Donation records: As required by Ghanaian tax regulations
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until you unsubscribe

9. Children's Privacy

Some of our programs serve students under 18. We collect information from minors only with appropriate consent and use it solely for program purposes. Parents or guardians may contact us to review or delete their child's information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when it was last revised. Significant changes will be communicated through our website or email.
